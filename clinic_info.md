## Prices
- Dermatology visit: 450 EGP
- Pediatrics visit: 400 EGP
- Internal medicine visit: 350 EGP
- Dental check-up: 300 EGP
- A follow-up visit within 14 days of the first visit is free.

## Insurance
We accept AXA, Allianz and MetLife cards. Bring your insurance card and your national ID to every visit.

## Booking and cancellation
- Book by phone or through the assistant.
- Please arrive 15 minutes before your appointment.
- Cancel at least 24 hours before the appointment. A late cancellation costs 100 EGP.

## Before your visit
- Blood tests: do not eat for 8 to 10 hours before the test. Water is allowed.
- Bring any previous test results and a list of the medicines you take.
