NILE FAMILY CLINIC - INSURANCE AND POLICIES
عيادة النيل للأسرة - التأمين والسياسات

INSURANCE
We accept AXA, Allianz and MetLife medical cards. Bring your insurance card and your national ID to every visit.
Lab tests and ultrasound need approval from your insurance company before the visit. Ask reception to send the request at least 2 working days before.
Dental cleaning is not covered by any of the three companies.
نقبل كروت التأمين الطبي من AXA و Allianz و MetLife. أحضر كارت التأمين والبطاقة الشخصية في كل زيارة.
التحاليل والسونار تحتاج موافقة شركة التأمين قبل الزيارة.

BOOKING, CANCELLATION AND LATE ARRIVAL
Book by phone or through the clinic assistant. Please arrive 15 minutes before your appointment.
Cancel or change your appointment at least 24 hours before it. A late cancellation costs 100 EGP.
If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, the visit may move to the next free slot.
A follow-up visit with the same doctor within 14 days of the first visit is free.
يجب إلغاء الموعد أو تغييره قبل 24 ساعة على الأقل. الإلغاء المتأخر يكلف 100 جنيه.
زيارة المتابعة مع نفس الطبيب خلال 14 يومًا مجانية.

PAYMENT
We accept cash and cards at reception. The assistant does not take payments.

PRIVACY
The clinic assistant stores only your name and phone number to manage your bookings. It never asks for your medical history.
لا يحفظ المساعد إلا اسمك ورقم هاتفك.

EMERGENCIES
The clinic is not an emergency service. In an emergency call 123 (ambulance) or go to the nearest hospital.
العيادة ليست قسم طوارئ. في حالة الطوارئ اتصل بـ 123.
