# Visiting the Clinic | زيارة العيادة

## Address and directions

Nile Family Clinic, 14 Central Axis, 6th of October City, Giza. Second floor, above the pharmacy. The nearest landmark is Al-Hosary Mosque, a 5-minute walk.

## Second opinions and medical reports

You can ask your doctor for a written medical report after the visit. A report costs 100 EGP and is ready in 2 working days. For a second opinion, bring all your previous reports and scans.

## Feedback and complaints

Tell the reception manager, or send an email to feedback@nileclinic.example. We answer within 3 working days.

## Children in the waiting area

The waiting area has a small kids' corner. Children must stay with an adult at all times.

## Languages

All doctors speak Arabic and English. Dr. Karim Hassan also speaks French.
