# Lab Test Catalogue | دليل التحاليل

Nile Family Clinic lab. Every test has a code: give the code to reception to book it. Results come by SMS with a link, or on paper from reception.

## L-101 Complete blood count (CBC) | صورة دم كاملة

Counts red cells, white cells and platelets. No fasting. Price 150 EGP. Result the same day after 4 PM.

## L-102 Fasting blood sugar (FBS) | سكر صائم

Measures blood sugar after fasting. Fast for 8 to 10 hours; water is allowed. Price 80 EGP. Result in 2 hours.

## L-103 HbA1c (average blood sugar) | السكر التراكمي

Shows the average blood sugar over the last 3 months. No fasting. Price 250 EGP. Result the next day.

## L-104 Lipid profile | دهون الدم

Cholesterol (LDL, HDL) and triglycerides. Fast for 8 to 10 hours. Price 300 EGP. Result the next day.

## L-201 Thyroid stimulating hormone (TSH) | هرمون الغدة الدرقية

Checks how the thyroid gland works. No fasting. Price 280 EGP. Result in 2 working days.

## L-202 Free T4 | هرمون T4 الحر

Usually ordered together with TSH. No fasting. Price 260 EGP. Result in 2 working days.

## L-301 Liver enzymes (ALT, AST) | إنزيمات الكبد

No fasting. Price 180 EGP. Result the same day.

## L-302 Kidney function (creatinine, urea) | وظائف الكلى

No fasting. Drink water as usual. Price 200 EGP. Result the same day.

## L-401 Vitamin D (25-OH) | فيتامين د

No fasting. Price 600 EGP. Result in 3 working days.

## L-402 Vitamin B12 | فيتامين ب12

No fasting. Price 450 EGP. Result in 3 working days.

## L-403 Ferritin (iron stores) | مخزون الحديد

No fasting. Price 350 EGP. Result the next day.

## L-501 Urine analysis | تحليل بول

Bring the first urine of the morning in the clean cup we give you. Price 70 EGP. Result the same day.

## L-601 Pregnancy test (beta hCG, blood) | تحليل حمل في الدم

No fasting. Price 220 EGP. Result in 3 hours.
